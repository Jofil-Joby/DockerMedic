## Decision and Reasoning

DockerMedic makes an assessment by analyzing evidence related to container configuration. It connects detected problems to supporting evidence and practical actions.

## Inputs and Data Sources

DockerMedic uses source files, configuration, project structure, and relevant container configuration data from the inspected project.

## Limits and Constraints

DockerMedic is limited when required information is missing, inaccessible, generated dynamically, or incomplete.
